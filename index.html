What is a granny flat?

A fully self-contained secondary dwelling, right in your own backyard.

A granny flat is a compact, self-contained home built on the same block as your existing house — complete with its own kitchen, bathroom, bedroom and private entrance. Under NSW planning rules, an approved granny flat can be legally rented out separately from the main house, making it one of the smartest ways to add income or living space to your property.

  • Fully self-contained: kitchen, bathroom & laundry included
  • Can be legally rented as a separate dwelling under NSW SEPP rules
  • Complying Development (CDC) fast-track approval available on most blocks
